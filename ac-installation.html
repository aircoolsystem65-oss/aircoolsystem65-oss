Frequently asked questions

Can you do piping before my flat is painted?

Yes. We do fresh copper piping and concealed in-wall piping for new flats so your walls stay clean.

Do you also uninstall and shift ACs?

Yes. We uninstall safely, help with packing and can reinstall at your new place.

Do you test the AC after installing?

Yes. Every installation includes vacuum, gas pressure and cooling checks before we leave.

What if extra piping or material is needed?

We measure first and tell you what is required before work starts. No surprises.

Which ACs do you install?

Split ACs of all major brands, including inverter models.
