Quick checks before you call

These simple steps sometimes solve the issue. If not, we are one call away.

Check power and MCB

Make sure the breaker and the plug point are on.

Check remote and mode

Replace remote batteries and confirm cool mode and temperature.

Rinse the filters

Dusty filters block airflow. Rinse them gently and dry before refitting.

Do not run a faulty AC

Ice or loud noise can damage the compressor. Switch off and call us.
