Frequently asked questions

How long does an AC service take?

A regular service takes about 45 to 60 minutes per AC. Jet cleaning may take a little longer.

Do I need jet cleaning or a regular service?

A regular service suits ACs that are cleaned every season. If the AC has not been serviced for long or smells bad, jet cleaning is better. Our technician will guide you.

How often should I service my AC?

Before summer and every 3 to 4 months if you use it daily. Homes with heavy dust may need more frequent cleaning.

Will you tell me the charges for gas or parts first?

Yes. Gas charging and parts, if needed, are explained and quoted before any work begins.

Do you service inverter and window ACs?

Yes, split, window and inverter ACs of all major brands.
