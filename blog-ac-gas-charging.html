Signs of low gas

Your AC runs all day but the room stays warm, ice forms on the pipe, or the outdoor pipe feels warm instead of cold. These are common signs of a gas problem.

  • Cooling is weak even after cleaning the filters
  • Ice on the copper pipe or indoor coil
  • Hissing or bubbling sound from the pipes
  • Electricity bill rising with poor cooling

Gas is never "used up"

AC gas does not finish on its own. If it is low, there is a leak. A good technician repairs the leak first and then refills gas, otherwise the problem returns in weeks.

Other reasons your AC may not cool

Before blaming gas, remember these common causes:

  • Dirty filters or a choked indoor coil
  • Faulty capacitor or fan motor
  • Blocked outdoor unit or poor ventilation
  • Wrong mode or temperature setting

What to do

Switch off the AC to protect the compressor and book a service. Our team checks pressure and cooling before suggesting any gas charging.

How we handle gas charging

  • Pressure and cooling test
  • Leak detection with a proper check
  • Leak repair before any refill
  • Correct gas quantity and final cooling test
