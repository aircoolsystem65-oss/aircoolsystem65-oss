Before you call us

A few quick checks that sometimes save a visit.

Check the water tap

Make sure the tap is open and the inlet pipe is not bent.

Clean the drain filter

A blocked filter is the most common reason for no draining.

Do not overload

Too many clothes strain the motor and cause noise and imbalance.

Close the lid fully

The machine will not start if the lid or door is not locked.

How much does a washing machine repair depend on?

It depends on the fault and the parts needed. The technician checks first and explains the repair before starting.

Do you repair front load machines?

Yes: front load, top load, fully automatic and semi-automatic.

Can you fix PCB problems at home?

Most PCB and motor issues are repaired at your home. Complex boards may need a short workshop visit, and we tell you upfront.

My machine is not draining. Is it serious?

Often it is a blocked filter or a drain pump issue. We clean or replace the part and check the rest of the system.
