Frequently asked questions

Do you repair Smart TVs?

Yes: LED, LCD, Plasma and Smart TVs of all major brands.

Is a home visit needed?

Most TV repairs are done at your home. If a part must go to the workshop, we tell you upfront.

My TV has sound but no picture. What is wrong?

Usually a backlight or power board problem. We test it and explain the repair before starting.

Can you help with Smart TV setup and wall mounting?

Yes. We help with Smart TV software, Wi-Fi and app issues and with installation.

Will you tell me the cost before replacing a panel or board?

Yes. Panel or board replacement is always explained and quoted before work starts.
